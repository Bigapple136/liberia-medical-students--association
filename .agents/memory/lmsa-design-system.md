---
name: AMSA-inspired LMSA design
description: The durable visual and content direction for the LMSA public website.
---

Use AMSA as a structural and editorial reference: help visitors choose an audience or goal first, use strong typographic hierarchy, modular storytelling, clear calls to action, and solid color fields instead of gradient-heavy decoration.

**Why:** This direction makes a medical student association feel purposeful and easier to navigate while keeping LMSA distinct through its own colors, voice, Liberia-focused content, and imagery.

**How to apply:** Keep the public site inspirational and content-led; keep the member portal practical and task-focused. Do not copy AMSA’s exact copy, imagery, logo treatment, or brand assets.
